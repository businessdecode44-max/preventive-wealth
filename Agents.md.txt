# Preventive Wealth - agents.md

## Brand Overview

Brand Name:
Preventive Wealth

Primary Tagline:
Financial Literacy as Prevention

Supporting Statement:
Helping individuals and families understand how money, protection, debt, taxes, and legacy work together before financial emergencies happen.

Website Goal:
Build a modern financial literacy ecosystem that educates, generates leads, sells digital educational products, nurtures trust, and supports long-term financial wellness through practical education.

Core Positioning:
Preventive Wealth positions financial literacy as a proactive form of prevention.

Financial literacy helps prevent:
- debt
- financial crisis
- generational poverty
- poor planning
- wealth loss
- family instability

Preventive Wealth is a financial education and wealth literacy platform first. Products and services support the educational ecosystem.

---

# Brand Philosophy

Many financial crises happen because people were never taught how money, protection, debt, taxes, and legacy actually work together.

Preventive Wealth exists to make financial literacy practical, understandable, and actionable before financial emergencies happen.

The brand focuses on:
- practical financial education
- family-centered financial wellness
- protection-based financial literacy
- generational thinking
- preventive financial behavior
- emotionally intelligent financial education

---

# Mission

To make financial literacy practical, understandable, and actionable for individuals and families before financial emergencies happen.

---

# Vision

To become a trusted global financial literacy platform helping families build stronger financial futures through education, prevention, and long-term wealth awareness.
